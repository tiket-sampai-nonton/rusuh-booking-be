B. Skenario uji

Menaikkan beban melewati kondisi normal untuk menemukan titik degradasi dan breaking point.

  • browse (ramping-arrival-rate): Light user: GET /events, /events/facets, /events/{id}, /queue-status/{id}
  • waiters (ramping-vus): Normal user: POST queue-entries (join), lalu heartbeat, GET queue-status dan /queue-entries/me berulang sesuai nextPollAfterMs
  • buy (ramping-arrival-rate): Heavy user: join, tunggu ADMITTED, claim, POST booking-sessions, POST bookings (hold 1 tiket), POST bookings/{id}/cancel
  • account (ramping-arrival-rate): User login: OTP request (email ke Mailpit), OTP verify, GET /me, GET /me/bookings, refresh, logout

Endpoint yang terlibat: booking.hold events.detail queue.me events.list booking.cancel events.facets queue.heartbeat user.otp.request session.create queue.status user.my-bookings user.refresh user.logout queue.join user.otp.verify queue.claim user.me
